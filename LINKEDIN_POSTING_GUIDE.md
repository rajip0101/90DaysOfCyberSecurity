# 📱 LinkedIn Posting Guide - 90 Days of Cybersecurity

Strategic guide for maintaining visibility and engagement during your 90-day cybersecurity journey!

---

## 🎯 Social Media Strategy Overview

**Posting Frequency**: 3-4 posts per week  
**Best Days**: Monday, Wednesday, Friday  
**Best Times**: 8 AM - 10 AM or 12 PM - 2 PM (Your Local Time)  
**Content Mix**: 40% Learning | 40% Progress | 20% Community/Motivation

---

## 📅 Weekly Posting Calendar

### Monday: Concept Breakdown
- Share a key concept from your week
- Include diagrams or explanations
- Highlight why it matters in real-world security
- **Hashtags**: #MondayLearning #CyberSecurity

### Wednesday: Lab/Exercise Completion
- Celebrate completing a challenge
- Share what you struggled with
- Explain your solution approach
- **Hashtags**: #WednesdayWin #HackerWednesday

### Friday: Weekly Recap + Next Week
- Summarize the week's learnings
- List 3 key takeaways
- Preview what's coming next week
- **Hashtags**: #FridayFeeling #WeeklyRecap

## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Be Authentic** - Share struggles AND wins
2. **Be Consistent** - 3-4 posts weekly without exception
3. **Be Helpful** - Answer others' questions
4. **Link GitHub** - Always reference your portfolio
5. **Use Visuals** - Posts with images get 20x more engagement
6. **Tell Stories** - Connect emotionally with your audience
7. **Network Actively** - Engage with others' posts daily
